About

Noel Edhagen is a student at The Florence Academy of Art, trained in the classical academic discipline and working between that tradition and contemporary design. Composition and design are central to his work. He has a strong pull toward both traditional fine art and modern graphic design, and his work sits at the intersection of the two, using classical academic technique to inform visually engaging, thoughtful digital and branded work.

Alongside his design work, Noel continues to draw and paint by hand, including portraits and still lifes worked in charcoal and oil paint.

That foundation carries into his design work, which spans digital design, visual layouts, branding materials, and creative content, built in Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, and InDesign.

Based in Gothenburg, Sweden, and always looking to grow the connection between fine art and contemporary design. Open to commissions in portraits and still lifes as well as graphic design.
